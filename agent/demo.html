1

Identify

Neo: shop owner on a phone. Nyumba: buyer who fears land fraud; seller who needs a clean listing.

2

Understand

Today: notebook, WhatsApp, brokers, classifieds. Pain: leaked credit, dead stock, deposits lost to scams.

3

Design value

Flagship jobs only: spoken sale → confirmed books. Sentence search + title walkthrough on that listing.

4

Deliver

Tools on ports. Stub data now, live backends at merge. Local brain if the model key is missing.

5

Capture

Confirm / discard by draft id. Auditable payload. Owner stays in control of money and listings.

6

Measure

Time to first confirmed sale. Search precision. Diligence completion. Evals on frozen utterances.

Customer

Neo Time-poor owner. Sales in their head. Credit given on trust. Opens the phone between customers.
Nyumba Will not sleep until the title is real. Will not use 12 filter checkboxes. Will not pay cash in a car park.

Value

Neo Afternoon back. Unpaid balances named. Low stock before the shelf is empty. Margin drift caught.
Nyumba Real matches in one sentence. Red flags on this plot. Seller price from comps, not vibes. Trust that closes.

Mechanism

Neo record_sale draft → Confirm. get_performance / get_outstanding / get_alerts read this shop only.
Nyumba search_listings reads now. run_due_diligence uses titleStatus. draft_listing + suggest_price wait for yes.
